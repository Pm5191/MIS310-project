Cybersecurity Threat Tracker

Welcome to the Cybersecurity Threat Tracker. This website helps beginning Cybersecurity students learn about common cyber threats, real-world security incidents, and ways to stay safe online.

Understanding Cyber Threats

A cyber threat is an action that could damage computer systems, steal information, or interrupt an organization's operations.

Phishing Awareness

Phishing attacks use misleading emails, messages, or websites to convince people to reveal information or open harmful content.
